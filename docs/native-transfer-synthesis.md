## Preparation and existing proposals

Null staff or buyer follows the original diagnostic and failure path. The
builder initializes proposal fields and packed flags in source order, while
preserving reserved bytes. An existing proposal can supply its fields in an
eligible club/mode path. Copies remain individual typed reads and writes;
replacing them with a bulk copy would change overlapping-object behavior.

Exchange candidates are filtered through their primary club, secondary contract,
contract class, staff category and other pending proposals. The output uses
8-byte exchange payloads. The copied status byte is cleared; two following
bytes are copied, while the final scratch byte retains its original prior state.
Empty lists are disposed and cleared. A separate path starts with the staff's
stored value and caps it against the finance service's signed low result word.

## Base, target and payment periods

The service can request a new output contract and use its shortcut path, or
resolve the primary club and calculate a new base and target through valuation
services. A second valuation can produce a difference used later in payment
composition. A contract clause can cap the initial base. Calls are retained even
where their repeated results look algebraically redundant: they can mutate
shared state or return a different value on the next query.

Payment-period selection considers a random threshold, two linked-object
selectors and signed finance mood codes. Buyer and primary-club factors differ.
The resulting factor can select codes 24, 18, 12 or 6 and rescale base, target
and limit through the original 0.6, 0.7, 0.8 or 0.9 divisors. These numeric codes
are not assigned unproved country, currency or duration labels.

## Composing a constrained proposal

If the amount limit is below the calculated base, a prior cap can directly
reduce the base and clear the period. Otherwise, the original 1.4 and 1.2
comparisons and valuation difference select mandatory or optional additional
components. Source category, signed cached age, availability, career reputation,
profile value and a club-policy service determine eligibility. Some combinations
refuse the proposal rather than merely lowering its price.

Ordinary installments, contingent amounts and a percentage component use
separate draws. For example, one contingent path stores the greater of 75 percent
of the constrained base and 85 percent of the valuation difference, before
truncation. Other paths use sampled percentages. The calculation and comparison
of two percentage draws can choose either their minimum or maximum, depending
on the original path. Cursor advancement can occur before the reseed call and
between arithmetic operations; that ordering is preserved explicitly.

The optional distribution also samples portions of the base and of source
terms, and can add a remaining component if all three are absent. Signed
remainders, wide-range correction sampling, integer wrapping, floating-point
status and single-precision rounding are kept where the original uses them.

## Refinement, refusal and finalization

The builder checks the best qualifying existing proposal, adjusts toward the
target, checks a full signed 64-bit finance result and can replace additional
components with a contractual cap. A later clause test uses 1.05 times the base;
when its condition holds, ordinary and contingent amounts and the percentage
are cleared and the base becomes the clause value.

Finalization considers the limit, current date, contract end date, eligibility
reason and packed state. An eligibility reason of 9 can allow the specific
otherwise-false result. Missing primary contracts and the mode byte can trigger
a final service valuation. These paths do not make transfer acceptance or the
whole negotiation subsystem complete.

Failure effects are part of the contract. In the output-contract constructor
failure path, the allocated object is freed but the output slot is left holding
its address. Other refusals explicitly clear that slot after disposal. The
reconstruction preserves this difference rather than silently adding rollback
or pointer repair.
